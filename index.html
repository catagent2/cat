What the contract can and cannot do

    
One small contract with no admin keys. Read the code on BscScan before you mint.

    

      
Cannot
  • Mint extra supply after deploy
  • Charge a tax or change the price
  • Pause, freeze or blacklist your wallet
  • Hold or withdraw your BNB

      
Be aware
  • The team holds 9.1% (100 million) from day one.
  • The contract creates no trading pool. The team adds liquidity by hand and no date is promised.
  • The contract was tested by its author, not by a professional audit firm.
  • No price, listing or profit is promised. You can lose what you pay.

    

  


  

    
Questions

    
Who can mint?
Any wallet, up to 10 mints each, until 10,000 wallets have minted. If the team switches on the Agent ID gate, only wallets holding that NFT can mint.

    
How much does it cost?
The price is fixed in BNB, about $0.03 per mint at launch, plus a small gas fee. It is fixed in BNB, so its dollar value changes with BNB. Ten mints give you 100,000 CATAGENT.

    
When can I trade it?
Only after the team adds liquidity on PancakeSwap. The mint contract does not do this. Announcements will be posted on X.

    
Is it safe to paste the command?
The command only contains the instructions link and the contract address. It never contains a key or a seed phrase. Never share your recovery phrase with anyone, including your agent.

    
Can I send BNB straight to the contract?
No. A plain transfer is rejected. Call mint with the exact price attached. Your AI agent does this for you.
